## E-Commerce & Retail Industry: Data Analysis

# Objective
We aim to conduct an Exploratory Data Analysis on an E-Commerce & Retail dataset and discover trends, patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, sales performance, ratings and relationships between variables.

# Overall Project Phases
# Phase 1: Data Collection
In this stage, we will be collecting our dataset and carrying out some preliminary inspections.
Activities:
-Collect our dataset.
-Carry out descriptive analysis.
-Identify key variables.
-Inspect the overall structure of the dataset.
-Carry out a check of columns and their data types.
-Check the first and last records.
-Generate summary statistics.

# Phase 2: Data Cleaning
In this stage, we will be cleaning our dataset to prepare it for analysis.
Activities:
-Carrying out preliminary checks and inspections to reveal data quality issues.
-Checking for missing values.
-Checking for duplicates.
-Removing duplicates.
-Handling missing values.
-Checking variable data types.
-Converting columns to appropriate formats.
-Renaming columns.
-Checking consistency in the dataset.

# Phase 3: Data Transformation
In this stage, we will carry out data transformation to prepare the dataset for analysis.
Activities:
-Carrying out transformations on variables.
-Converting date-time columns to date columns where necessary.
-Renaming variables where necessary.
-Preparing numerical variables for analysis.

# Phase 4: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
EDA involves summarizing and inspecting the data to reveal trends, patterns, relationships, and interesting findings in the dataset.
Univariate Analysis
This involves the analysis of one variable at a time.
Visualization:
-Count plot
-Histogram
-Box plot
-Distribution plot
Variables analysed:
-Gender
-Product category
-Customer segment
-Sales
-Profit
-Rating
-Delivery days
The project carries out descriptive analysis on numerical variables such as Sales, Rating and Delivery Days.

# Bivariate Analysis
This involves the analysis of two variables at a time.
Visualization:
-Bar plot
-Scatter plot
-Box plot
-Heatmap
Analysis carried out:
-Category v/s Sales
-Category v/s Profit
-Quantity v/s Profit
-Discount v/s Sales
-Shipping method v/s Delivery days
Analysis of relationship between numerical variables.

# Multivariate Analysis
This involves the analysis of multiple variables.
Activity:
-Analysis of relationship between multiple variables.
-Trend discovery.
-Carrying out correlation analysis.
-Analysis of relationship between sales, profit, discount, quantity, days and other numerical variables.

# Phase 5: Data Visualization
In this stage, we will carry out data visualization to highlight key insights, trends, and patterns.
Activities:
-Visualizing trends and patterns.
-Comparing categories.
-Making deductions and drawing conclusions.
-Creating bar charts, count plots, histograms, box plots, scatter plots, heatmap, violin plots etc.
# Phase 6: Analysis Report
The project analyses various aspects of the E-Commerce & Retail dataset.
These include:
-Customer Analysis
We carry out customer analysis to discover trends, patterns and behaviour in customer segments. We also compare the various customer segments with each other to find differences and similarities.
-Sales Analysis
With sales analysis, we are able to discover and report sales trends, patterns, and relationships.
-Product Analysis
Here, we analyse product categories to examine differences in performance and discover patterns, trends and relationships in product sales, product category, and profit.
-Rating Analysis
This section looks at the customer rating data and attempts to discover patterns, trends, and relationships in the ratings data.
-Category Analysis
We analyse the different categories to compare category sales and profit.
-Correlation Analysis
We carry out a correlation analysis to discover and report relationships between numerical variables. We look at relationships between sales, profit, discount, quantity, days, and other variables.

# Trends & Patterns
In this section, we discuss and report the general trends and patterns discovered in the analysis.
Differences between categories and variables are highlighted.

# Phase 7: Key Insights
We carry out key insights to discover and report key patterns, trends, relationships, and differences in the sales, product category, customer segments, ratings, profit, and other variables in the study.
Key areas of focus in this project:
-Sales performance.
-Performance of products and categories.
-Customer behaviour.
-Customer ratings.
-Profit trends and patterns.
-Relationships between variables.
-Improvement opportunities.
-The project makes several recommendations in relation to opportunity areas.
It also highlights recommendations in relation to high-selling categories, delivery days, customer rewards and discount policies.

# Phase 8: Conclusion
The conclusion section summarizes the major highlights of the study, including data preparation, sales, product, customer, rating, correlation, and other insights.
It also highlights opportunities for improvement and gives final recommendations.
The conclusion highlights the importance of this study to understand customer behaviour and make informed decisions regarding products, sales, and other factors.
